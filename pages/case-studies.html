At a glance

Different journeys. Practical plans.

01

The daily neighborhood loop

A family needs reliable morning and afternoon coverage. Check school timing, stop access, seat capacity and collection arrangements.

Evidence: route assignment and handoff record
02

The after-school activity

Students leave later after clubs or tutoring. Align dismissal, passenger list, late-route timing and guardian communication.

Evidence: approved activity timetable and passenger list
03

The educational visit

A school group travels to a destination and returns together. Confirm capacity, chaperones, accessibility and dispatch.

Evidence: trip brief, manifest and return plan
What useful proof looks like

Records that answer real questions.

Route records

Approved schools, stops, calendars and assignment changes.

Fleet records

Vehicle inspection summaries and maintenance status.

People records

Driver credentials, training and assigned responsibilities.

Journey records

Boarding, arrival, service exceptions and follow-up actions.
