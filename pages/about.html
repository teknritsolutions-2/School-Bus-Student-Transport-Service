About WAYBELL

A clearer connection between home, bus, and school.

WAYBELL is a complete demonstration of a parent-focused school transport experience, designed around confidence and clarity.

Our purpose

Turn the daily journey into useful, understandable information.

School transport depends on trust. Our template shows how route details, journey progress, student assignments, plan management, and support can work together in one consistent interface.

The names, routes, prices, and operational claims shown here are demonstration content and should be replaced before production use.

What guides us

Practical values for every screen.

✓

Safety first

Important information should be visible, legible, and calm.

◇

Transparency

Routes, prices, and states use direct, useful language.

↔

Accessibility

Keyboard focus, responsive layouts, dark mode, and RTL support are included.

◎

Consistency

Shared navigation and styles keep every page familiar.

A simple operating model

From route setup to parent updates.

  1. Define routes and stops

    Record the path, schedule, school, and neighborhood coverage.

  2. Assign student profiles

    Connect each student to a route and preferred boarding stop.

  3. Share journey information

    Present location progress, travel events, and service messages.
