The student safety guide

Familiar habits. More confident journeys.

Review the stop, boarding signal, seating instructions and collection arrangement together.

From waiting to arrival

Practice the whole journey.

  1. Pickup stop

    A familiar place, an agreed time.

  2. Boarding

    Wait for the signal, then step aboard.

  3. School arrival

    A coordinated handoff at school.

  4. Afternoon return

    The same care on the way home.

At the stop

Wait where the driver can see you.

Before boarding

Use the assigned stop, arrive in time and stay away from the vehicle approach. Wait for the bus to stop and the driver or attendant to signal.

During the ride

Use the assigned seat and any required restraint. Keep bags contained, the aisle clear and exits unobstructed.

When leaving

Follow the handrail and exit instructions. Move to the agreed collection area and follow the authorized handoff.

If plans change

Do not follow or approach a moving bus after a missed pickup. Use the operator’s confirmed support channel.

Keep verified contacts close.

For immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.
